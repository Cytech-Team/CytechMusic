## Music Commands (music.py)
1. /join - Joins your voice channel
2. /leave (disconnect, dc) - Disconnect the bot from voice channel
3. /play [query] (pla, p) - Play music from any source
4. /nowplaying (np) - Show the currently playing song
5. /volume [1-200] (vol, v) - Set music volume
6. /pause (pa, pau, res, unpau) - Pause/Resume playback
7. /skip (ski, sk) - Skip current track
8. /skipto [position] (jump) - Skip to specific track
9. /clear (cl) - Clear the queue
10. /stop (sto, st) - Stop playback and disconnect
11. /seek [time] - Seek to specific time
12. /queue (qu, que) - Show current queue
13. /remove [position] (rm) - Remove track from queue
14. /shuffle (sh) - Shuffle the queue
15. /lyrics (ly) - Show lyrics for current song
16. /grab (save, yoink) - Save current track to DM
17. /removedupes (rmd, dedupe) - Remove duplicate tracks
18. /move [from] [to] (mv) - Move track in queue
19. /history (played) - Show recently played songs
